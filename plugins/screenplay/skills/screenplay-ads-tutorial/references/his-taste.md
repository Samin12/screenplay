# His taste: everything Samin said during the Claude Ads edit (Nov 24 2026)

A checklist. Read it before planning and again before every hand-back.

Four kinds of lines:
- **In quotation marks**: his words, as they reached the editor (lightly cleaned dictation).
- **(relayed)**: the boss's log of a note from him. The meaning is his, the wording is not.
- **(via the parent)**: a note that reached the boss through the session he works in. Treat it as his.
- **(editor's rule)**: a rule the editors or reviewers made while serving his notes. Not his words.

Every rule he gave is quoted once, here. The other reference files point back to this one.

## The ask

- [ ] "Edit the entire video like a screen, using the skill, such that every idea I talk about is actually
  shown on the computer: dragging, typing things in, putting prompts in as I'm talking. Do all the cuts and
  make a fully edited video. Wherever applicable, when I'm talking about something, really create the scenes
  and show them. Make sure the assets I gave you are fully shown in each level."
- [ ] What he liked at the end, and wants kept: "I really like how you move my camera in and out, how you
  highlight everything, the chapter titles, everything like that. And how you created the Claude app and did
  the things there. All of these things are great. Make sure to save these kinds of assets so we can create
  and edit videos like this really fast."

## Story and flow

- [ ] "The edit is good, but some parts are not flowing logically. With my voice and the things I say, make
  sure we're cutting in and out, recombining sentences, moving it around if we need to, and cutting
  superlatives and all these other things, so everything flows naturally."
- [ ] "There are some connecting words missing that make it a bit jarring… Like at 0:05, I would like to have
  something like 'Because Claude can now…'"
- [ ] "Cutting out the 4 steps portion will make more sense." The whole "four steps" overview chapter went.
  (editor's reading) One point, once: the hook already said it and each level walks the steps anyway.
- [ ] "This point and section isn't smooth, it jarringly cuts to 'and level three'… is there nowhere I say
  next level or level 2?" (on a join left by trimming "like this one"). (editor's rule) Smooth flow beats
  the trim rule: keep the breath at every trim join, and put the phrase back when the trim can be heard.
  He wants each level named out loud and on screen. If he never said
  it, it goes on the pickup list; it is never synthesized.

## Proof

- [ ] "Use the proof-first video skill. When we're saying Claude can make motion graphics, we should show
  examples from the internet that people have built for motion graphics. And vice versa for a lot of other
  things. We want to show proof of everything and all the claims we make."
- [ ] Never the community price on screen. (standing rule)
- [ ] No tool counts or numbers he did not say and no source backs. (editor's rule, from a review)

## The intro hook

- [ ] "clearly show the cool AI Grandmaster videos."
- [ ] "When you're showing the videos in the intro, make sure they're actually in frame and we're seeing
  the most impactful part." Clips play whole and big, each on its best moment. No tiny grid of thumbnails.
- [ ] (relayed) Cut the filler pointer phrase when it adds nothing, but see "smooth flow" above.
- [ ] (relayed) For the win, show the award footage, not his submissions, in this order: handover, the press
  headline, the finalists.
- [ ] (via the parent) Award clips and the press article fill at least 85% of the frame width.
- [ ] (via the parent) Recreated Claude windows are framed with the camera. No big white areas.
- [ ] (via the parent) Maybe one more beat of him, 2 to 3 s, where the words allow it.
- [ ] (relayed) The levels are unclear in the hook. (editor's rule) A level chip on each level's sentence.

## Layout

- [ ] "If there are points where you think it doesn't make sense for me to be on camera, just show the full
  thing full screen. That's fine too. Or if there are points where it makes sense for me to just be talking in
  full screen, put that in as well."

## Showcase clips

- [ ] (editor's rule) The finished piece for each level plays in full, with its own sound, labelled as what

## Chapter cards

- [ ] "For some reason the chapters have no little gaps where we can take a breath, like the screenplay
  skill does it. Make sure to show that progression, and have those chapter titles embedded as well, and the
  chapter headings." (via the parent) In Borumi each card is its own scene named "Card · title".
- [ ] "You don't have to reveal the full thing. It could be, for example, a line that's traversing through,
  only taking you to the next step, and maybe we can just see the stuff before it. Other stuff can stay
  locked, like we do in the screenplay." So: done chapters dimmed with their titles, the current one revealed
  by a line that travels one step, upcoming ones locked with no title.

## Sound

- [ ] "there are a lot of whoosh sounds, remove all of them." Clicks and pops only, plus his chapter click.
- [ ] (editor's rule, from a review) No dead digital silence in a breather. Clean room tone.

- [ ] Never write to his own project. Never delete. Never publish. (standing rules)
