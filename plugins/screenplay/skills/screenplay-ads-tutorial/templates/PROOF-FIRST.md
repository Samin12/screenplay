His words: "<paste his note on proof, word for word>"

- **One proof pack for the whole video: `W/proof/`** (`README.md`, `proof-index.json`, every capture with
  its `.source.json`, a do-not-show list, `VERIFY.md`). Re-read `W/proof/README.md` before you finalise.
- **On screen**: a `stage` image with `url` + `source` ("domain · captured <date>"), the `-crop.png`, a
  highlight landing on the spoken word, on the exact words making the claim.
- **Proof beats recreation for any claim about the world.** Recreation is for "here is what you type and
  what you get back". Use both: proof on the claim, the recreated screen on the doing.
- Clips of other people's work: only the verifier's safe seconds, muted, credited, small.
- A claim with no proof in the pack: do not illustrate it as fact. Keep the screen on something true and
  list it in your handoff under "Unproven claims", with the proof you wish existed.
- Never a capture on the do-not-show list. Never the community price. Never an account name or email.
